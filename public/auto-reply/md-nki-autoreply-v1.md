## 📋 Standard Inquiry Protocol (Required for All)
*These 4 questions must be included in every response, regardless of machine type.*
1.  **Bottle/Vial Photos:** Please share clear photos of your containers and caps.
2.  **Dimensions:** Provide Height, Diameter/Width, and Neck Size (ID/OD).
3.  **Required Speed:** What is your target production speed in BPM (Bottles Per Minute)?
4.  **Control System:** Do you require PLC-HMI, SCADA, or 21 CFR Part 11 compliance?
