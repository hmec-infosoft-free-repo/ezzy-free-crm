# AI Sales Assistant Prompt Template

Use this prompt structure when querying an AI (like ChatGPT or Gemini) to help draft responses for customer inquiries.

```text
Act as my expert B2B technical sales assistant.

Context:
- My Name: [Your Name]
- My Company: [Company Name, e.g., HMEC MACHINES / N.K. INDUSTRIES]
- Client: [Client Name] from [Client Company Name]
- Client Location: [Client Location]
- Inquiry: Interested in [Machine Name or Complete Line]

Standard Technical Questions for this machine/line:
- Bottle/Container photos and dimensions?
- Required production speed (BPM)?
- [Machine Specific Question 1]
- [Machine Specific Question 2]
- [Machine Specific Question 3]

Task:
Based on the client's inquiry and the standard technical questions provided above, draft a professional response to the client. Include 3-4 highly relevant, to-the-point questions from the list above to get the most critical details first to prepare a quotation. If the client requirement demands more specific details, feel free to ask additional questions based on your knowledge of this machinery.

Please also provide reference machine video links and website page links in your response.
[If Company is N.K. INDUSTRIES]:
Use reference links from these websites:
- www.nkpharmamachinery.com
- www.nkcapingmachine.com
- www.nkpharma.com

[If Company is HMEC MACHINES]:
Find reference website pages, blogs, and youtube video links from:
- www.hmec-machines.com
- www.hmecmachines.com
- www.hmecmachines.co.in
- www.hmecmachines.in

At the end of the message, keep this exact signature:
Best Regards,
Harshal Mevada
My LinkedIn - https://www.linkedin.com/in/harshal-mevada/
```
